# AVENQRA - User Manual & Operating Guide

Welcome to the AVENQRA Civic Issue Resolution System manual. This guide provides operating instructions for Citizens, Field Officers, and Administrators.

---

## 📱 Mobile App (Citizens & Field Officers)

### Registering an Account
1. Open the AVENQRA App on your Android or iOS device.
2. Select **Register**, enter your Name, Email, Password, and Phone Number.
3. Tap **Sign Up** to create your citizen profile.

### Filing a Civic Complaint
1. Tap the **"+" Report Issue** button on the bottom navigation bar.
2. Take a clear photo of the infrastructure issue (e.g. pothole or overflowing bin).
3. Allow location permissions to capture exact GPS coordinates.
4. Verify the auto-detected category or select manually.
5. Add an optional description and tap **Submit Complaint**.

---

## 💻 Web Admin & Officer Dashboard

### Logging In
1. Open `http://localhost:3000` in your web browser.
2. Enter your Department or Admin credentials.
3. Access the central dashboard view.

### Dispatching Officers & Resolving Tickets
1. Go to **Complaints Management**.
2. Filter by status: `SUBMITTED`.
3. Select a ticket, view the AI severity score and attached photo.
4. Click **Assign Officer** and select an available field officer.
5. Once resolved by the officer, review resolution media and mark status as `RESOLVED`.
